VPN Protection

For continuous protection, configure Android's system VPN safeguards for Portmaster.

1. Tap the next to Portmaster.
2. Enable "Always-on VPN" so Android restarts Portmaster after reboot or unexpected service termination.
Strongly recommended for leak protection: enable "Block connections without VPN". This is Android's system-level kill switch: if Portmaster is not established, Android blocks network access instead of silently falling back to your real connection. Apps deliberately excluded from Portmaster may also lose network access while lockdown is enabled.
